About SB SRMSCET

The IEEE Student Branch allows students to connect, build projects, and learn from seniors, faculty, and industry experts. By joining, students gain access to regional conferences, technical workshops, global competitions, and a network of over 400,000 members worldwide.

Through initiatives like S-PACs (Student Professional Awareness Conferences) and project fund sponsorships, we help bridge the gap between academic theory and industry engineering practices.
